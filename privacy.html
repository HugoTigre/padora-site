Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

This policy describes the Padora Android companion app (“the app”). Padora is developed by odd.tiger (contact below).

Summary

  • Match history is stored on your phone only.
  • There is no cloud account and no Padora backend server in the current version.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • Sync from your watch uses the Zepp app on your phone and a localhost connection on your device — data does not leave your phone except through that local sync path.

Data the app stores on your device

When you sync from your watch, the app may store:

  • Match date and time
  • Set scores and match result (winner or draw)
  • Internal match identifiers used for sync and delete

This data stays in the app’s local storage on your phone until you delete it in the app.

Watch pairing

If you set up watch pairing, a 6-digit pairing code is stored on your phone using Android encrypted storage (EncryptedSharedPreferences). It is used only to verify sync requests on your device. It is not sent to Padora servers (there are none).

What we do not collect

  • No Padora user account
  • No analytics or crash reporting SDKs in the current version
  • No collection of name, email, or location by the app
  • No automatic upload of your match history to our servers

Third-party services

Sync requires the Zepp mobile app and a compatible smartwatch running the Padora watch app. Data flows watch → Zepp → your phone’s Padora app over a local connection on the device. We do not control Zepp’s privacy practices; see Zepp’s policies for their app.

Backups and security

Android cloud backup for the app is disabled. Match history and pairing data remain on your device unless you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Your choices

  • Delete individual matches in the app
  • Stop sync and remove the app to delete local data
  • Regenerate or clear pairing in Sync → Manage pairing

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes (for example, if we add cloud features). The “Last updated” date at the top will change accordingly.
